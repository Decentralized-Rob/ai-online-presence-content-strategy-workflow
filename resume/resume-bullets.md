# Resume Framing

Use this project as evidence of research, AI operations, content strategy, SEO thinking, and workflow design.

## Resume bullet options

- Built a public-source online presence audit workflow that maps a company's website, search visibility, content gaps, customer-facing friction points, and AI/automation opportunities into a reusable implementation plan.

- Developed a repeatable research and content strategy system using public sources, source-of-truth notes, prompt templates, and structured outputs to identify SEO, content, and automation opportunities for a target company.

- Created a GitHub-documented workflow showing how AI-assisted research can turn public company information into practical content, SEO, and operational improvement recommendations without relying on private data.

## Short project title

AI-assisted public presence audit and implementation workflow

## Interview framing

I built this as a transparent example of how I approach a company from the outside: research what is public, document the source trail, identify gaps, and turn that into practical execution ideas. The value is not a single prompt. It is the repeatable process and judgment around what to research, what to ignore, and how to turn findings into useful work.
