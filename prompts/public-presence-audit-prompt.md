# Public Presence Audit Prompt

Use this prompt to review a company's public online presence without claiming internal access.

```text
Review the public online presence of [COMPANY]. Use only public sources. Separate verified facts, observations, and assumptions.

Capture:
1. Official website structure
2. Main products or services
3. Search visibility and obvious SEO gaps
4. Customer support and FAQ coverage
5. Social and community presence
6. Public customer questions or complaints
7. Competitor examples worth comparing
8. Practical content, SEO, automation, or AI opportunities

Rules:
- Do not claim affiliation.
- Do not infer internal strategy.
- Cite sources where possible.
- Treat forum and Reddit comments as sentiment, not verified fact.
- Keep recommendations practical and tied to observed gaps.
```
