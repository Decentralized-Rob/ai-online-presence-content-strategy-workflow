# AI Online Presence Content Strategy Workflow

This repo documents an independent research workflow for reviewing a company's public online presence and turning that research into practical content, SEO, automation, and AI implementation ideas.

It is not client work. It does not use private access, insider information, paid tools, or confidential material. Everything here should be based on public sources, documented observations, and clearly labeled assumptions.

## What this is

A simple operating file for showing how I research a company, organize source material, identify missed opportunities, and turn that into usable execution ideas.

The goal is not to make a polished pitch deck. The goal is to show the work: what was found, what it means, what could be improved, and what I would do next.

## Repo structure

- `source-of-truth/` — verified notes, links, and source material
- `research/` — public online presence audit notes
- `strategy/` — practical opportunities and implementation ideas
- `prompts/` — reusable prompts used to structure the work
- `outputs/` — sample content, SEO, and automation outputs
- `resume/` — resume-ready project framing

## Boundaries

- No private company data
- No scraped personal data
- No claims of affiliation
- No claim that the company approved or requested the work
- Public research only
- Speculative ideas labeled as speculative

## Current example

The first test case is based on public research around Kah Motor / Honda Singapore. This repo should stay generic enough that the same workflow can be reused for other companies later.
