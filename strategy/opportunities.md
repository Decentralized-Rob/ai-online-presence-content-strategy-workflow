# Opportunities

These are implementation ideas based on public research. They are not claims about internal company needs.

## Content opportunities

- Improve pages that answer common ownership questions.
- Build clearer pages for service, warranty, financing, insurance, and maintenance questions.
- Create model-specific comparison and ownership content.
- Turn reviews and public materials into better structured search pages.

## SEO opportunities

- Map high-intent search terms to existing or missing pages.
- Strengthen title tags, headings, FAQs, and internal links.
- Add structured FAQ-style content where appropriate.
- Build pages around real customer search behavior rather than generic brand copy.

## AI and automation opportunities

- Public-facing FAQ assistant based only on approved site content.
- Service booking support flow.
- Ownership question triage.
- Content repurposing workflow for reviews, model updates, and after-sales information.
- Internal draft assistant for turning public materials into web, email, and social copy.

## Customer friction areas to investigate

- Test drive booking
- Service appointment booking
- Warranty questions
- Pricing clarity
- Hybrid/EV education
- After-sales expectations
- Location-specific questions

## Priority framework

1. Start with pages tied to revenue or customer friction.
2. Use public questions and search behavior to decide what content should exist.
3. Build small, useful improvements before proposing large systems.
4. Keep every recommendation traceable to a source or clear observation.
